Tropical Care Protocol

Healing Your Ink In Bali Climate

Bali's heat and humidity require specific precautions. Follow our simple 3-phase guide to help your tattoo heal well.

Phase 1: First Few Days

Keep It Clean & Dry

Your artist will cover your new tattoo and explain when to remove the covering. For the first few days, keep the area clean, dry and protected.

  • Strictly NO swimming in the ocean at Kuta Beach, pools, or waterfalls.
  • Quick lukewarm showers are fine; do not soak or submerge your tattoo.
  • Avoid direct tropical sun exposure on the tattooed limb.
Phase 2: Week 1 to 2

Gentle Warm Water Washes

Wash your tattoo gently with clean warm water and mild fragrance-free soap, then pat dry with a fresh paper towel. Keep it simple and let your skin heal naturally.

  • Wash gently with warm water 2-3 times daily, using clean hands.
  • Never scratch or pick peeling skin flakes or dry scabs.
  • Wear loose, breathable cotton or linen clothing.
Phase 3: Week 2 Onwards

Long-Term UV Preservation

Your surface skin is reformed, but deeper dermis tissue is solidifying. Protect your investment against tropical UV discoloration.

  • Protect your healed tattoo with SPF 50+ sunscreen outdoors in Bali.
  • Swimming in the ocean or pool can resume once fully healed.
  • Any questions while healing? Message us on WhatsApp anytime.

Everything You Need To Know

Clear answers about swimming in Bali, walk-ins, payments, and custom tattoo preparation.

You must avoid submerging your fresh tattoo in ocean water, hotel swimming pools, or waterfalls for at least 10 to 14 days. Ocean water contains natural marine bacteria that can trigger serious infections in open skin, and pool chlorine bleaches fresh pigment. We strongly recommend scheduling your tattoo toward the end of your Bali trip so you can enjoy surfing and swimming beforehand!

Walk-ins are warmly welcomed daily between 10:00 AM and 8:00 PM for smaller pieces, flash designs, and script work based on immediate chair availability. For large custom projects and sleeves, we recommend booking in advance through our enquiry form or WhatsApp to guarantee your preferred time.

We accept Indonesian Rupiah (IDR Cash), Visa, Mastercard, Wise transfers, and Australian Bank Transfers (PayID/BSB). Credit card terminals incur standard bank merchant surcharges (approx. 2.5–3%).
