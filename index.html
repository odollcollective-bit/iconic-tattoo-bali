Tropical Care Protocol

Healing Your Ink In Bali Climate

Bali's heat and humidity require specific precautions. Follow our clinical 3-phase timeline for vivid, lifelong ink saturation.

Phase 1: Days 1 to 4

The Second-Skin Barrier

Keep your medical Dermalize Pro bandage on for 3 to 4 days. It creates an optimal moist healing environment using your body's natural fluids.

  • Strictly NO swimming in Canggu ocean, pools, or waterfalls.
  • Quick lukewarm showers are safe; do not submerge bandage.
  • Avoid direct tropical sun exposure on the tattooed limb.
Phase 2: Days 5 to 10

Gentle Cleansing & Hydration

Gently peel off the film under running water. Clean with fragrance-free antibacterial soap, pat dry with fresh paper towel, and apply a micro-layer of aftercare balm.

  • Moisturize 2-3 times daily with sheer organic tattoo butter.
  • Never scratch or pick peeling skin flakes or dry scabs.
  • Wear loose, breathable cotton or linen clothing.
Phase 3: Days 11 to 28+

Long-Term UV Preservation

Your surface skin is reformed, but deeper dermis tissue is solidifying. Protect your investment against tropical UV discoloration.

  • Apply Mineral Zinc SPF 50+ whenever outdoors in Bali.
  • Swimming in ocean / pool can now resume safely.
  • Complimentary touch-ups within 3 months if needed.

You must avoid submerging your fresh tattoo in ocean water, hotel swimming pools, or waterfalls for at least 10 to 14 days. Ocean water contains natural marine bacteria that can trigger serious infections in open skin, and pool chlorine bleaches fresh pigment. We strongly recommend scheduling your tattoo toward the end of your Bali trip so you can enjoy surfing and swimming beforehand!
